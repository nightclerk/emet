אמת Emet

In Prague, the story goes, Rabbi Loew shaped a man from river clay and wrote אמת, truth, on its forehead. Erase the first letter and it reads מת, dead, and the clay is only clay again.

This golem is shaped from you. It breathes when you breathe, its heart glows with your pulse, and its face follows yours. The א on its forehead holds only while you stay calm.

What happens when you wake it
  • Your camera (or a video file you choose) is read frame by frame on this computer by the Presage SDK. Nothing is recorded or saved, and the golem never shows your face.
  • The only network traffic is the SDK checking your Presage key. Anonymous SDK diagnostics are off unless you turn them on below.
  • "Calm" here is a playful comparison with your own first minute, not a measurement of health. This is not a medical device.
  • Stop at any time with Return to clay: the camera turns off and every reading is dropped.

Use only video of someone who has agreed to it.
